    what to do next

    meet dogs. ask better questions.

    Use each breed card’s direct profile and the complete 205-breed catalog as starting points. Then meet adult examples where possible, discuss your real week with the provider, check housing and insurance rules, and verify health records. An adult foster dog’s known routine may be more useful than a puppy prediction.

    The matching approach follows current guidance to consider lifestyle, activity, training, grooming, time, and the individual animal. Read the underlying guidance from American Kennel Club, RSPCA, Blue Cross, and OFA/CHIC.

    can I use it when adopting a rescue?

    Yes. Use the breeds as background research, but give more weight to the rescue or foster team’s evidence about that dog’s home behavior, needs, and history.
