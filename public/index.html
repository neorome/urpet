    method before magic

    requirements in. tradeoffs out.

    The first release uses reviewed RSPCA welfare guidance and a CDC reptile-health gate. It screens what you say you can provide against specific profiles. It does not diagnose health, replace an experienced veterinarian, or promise that an individual animal will behave like a category.

    why are some obvious pets missing?

    Tropical community tanks, bettas, large parrots, aquatic turtles, crested geckos, ball pythons, and many other popular animals remain marked “researching.” Their social, environmental, legal, or veterinary needs differ too much to borrow another species’ profile.

    does a research lead mean “recommended”?

    No. It means the profile did not conflict with the limits you chose. Verify the individual animal, source, local law, housing rules, veterinary access, full cost, and backup care before deciding.
